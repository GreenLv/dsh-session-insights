On 2026-11-06 the maintainer made a new, limited decision accepting the known upstream risk for 0.8.0 and the exact reviewed rc.1 cohort: 9 bound files, the host queue, and the two reviewed 18-entry graph variants (primary + same-closure alternate enumeration). Policy at `config/dependency-audit-exceptions.json`.
- Local fresh scans (root lock with rc.1 peers): production and full each exited **1** with the single advisory and 18 affected entries; both report graphs share digest `b56c1216…`; the reviewed variant digest is `4c0bfa1d…`. Policy-aware assessment classified both scopes `accepted_known_risk`; the summary gate reported `passed_with_known_upstream_risk` with `exception_fixed: false`. These are local results, not exact-commit CI results; CI re-runs the same gate on the final commit.
- Python audit (`pip-audit` over the declared set): exit 0, "No known vulnerabilities found", 3 declared dependency specifications.
- Fail-closed properties retained: scan failure, invalid JSON, empty inventory, cohort/queue drift, unknown advisories and unreviewed graphs cannot pass; regression tests cover the rc.1 graph, its variant, and drift for the new cohort.
